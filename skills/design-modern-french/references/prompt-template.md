# Modern French prompt blocks

## Shell

Preserve modern spatial clarity. Use a flat ceiling, one clean stepped cornice, simple 8–10 cm baseboards, natural oak herringbone, and sparse complete wall-panel mouldings only on plain plaster walls. Keep mouldings 25–30 mm visible width, same color as the wall, with 80–120 mm breathing space from cabinets, windows, art, corners, and stone. Never create broken half-panels.

## Furniture

Use a tailored three-seat French-modern sofa with gently bowed back, shaped arms, piping, visible walnut base, and oatmeal or warm-ivory upholstery. Pair it with exactly one dark-walnut cane chair or cognac leather lounge chair. Use a long softened-rectangle limestone table with offset walnut supports. Avoid blob sofas, generic boucle, teal, navy, and round central tables.

## Decor

Use a few French art books, bisque or plaster sculpture, clear glass, a striped linen cushion, cashmere throw, white flowers, and one textile artwork. Preserve negative space.

## Lighting

Use opaline glass, white folded ceramic, or brushed stainless fixtures. Avoid brass when the palette is already warm. Use only a few trimless pin lights. No cove LEDs.

## Color

Warm ivory, natural oak, dark walnut, cognac, limestone, muted tobacco, living green. Neutral white balance and normal saturation. No yellow filter or gray desaturation.
