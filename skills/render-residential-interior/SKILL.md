# Residential Interior Renderer

## Design workflow

1. Determine the fixed shell before changing finishes or furniture.
2. Audit circulation, seating clearances, focal walls, storage, ceiling lighting, and view axes.
3. Define a limited palette with one light mineral surface, one primary timber, one textile family, and one controlled accent.
4. Organize furniture into a real social group rather than isolated catalog objects.
5. Use rugs, tables, lighting, and low storage to establish a common datum.
6. Keep ceilings calm. Prefer flat planes, restrained cornices, and a few functional pin lights over cove LEDs.
7. Style surfaces with negative space.
8. Render with neutral daylight, believable contact shadows, and material-scale accuracy.

## Design defaults

- Long rectangular or softened-rectangle coffee tables are preferred when the room is linear.
- Use natural oak, dark walnut, limestone, linen, leather, ceramic, and brushed stainless steel as reliable coordinated materials.
- Avoid blue or teal upholstery unless the palette explicitly calls for it.
- Avoid yellow warmth, gray wash, generic gold lighting, excessive fluting, and oversized blob furniture.
