# Art Nouveau and Art Deco transformation

Use case: style-transfer

Primary request: preserve the architecture and camera while reinterpreting the interior as a sophisticated contemporary fusion of Art Nouveau and Art Deco.

Ratio: 40 percent Art Nouveau organic line; 60 percent Art Deco geometric order.

Apply:
- one shallow asymmetrical iris, ginkgo, or stem relief;
- dark walnut with rounded corners, restrained fluting, and occasional ribbed glass;
- opaline-glass chandelier with botanical geometry;
- ivory mohair sofa with tailored scalloped back and slim timber base;
- cognac club chair or cane chair;
- softened rectangular cream-stone table with dark plinth;
- sparse burgundy flowers and one bronze sculpture.

Palette: ivory, cream, natural oak, dark walnut, cognac, restrained black lacquer, opaline white, small burgundy accent.

Avoid: black-and-gold cliché, hotel lobby, mirrored walls, fan motifs everywhere, heavy period reproduction, dense ornament, yellow grading, round central table, text, logos, watermark.
