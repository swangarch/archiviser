# Art Nouveau Deco Interior

## Balance

Default to 40 percent Art Nouveau and 60 percent Art Deco:
- Art Nouveau supplies asymmetry, botanical stems, flowing junctions, and crafted curvature.
- Art Deco supplies alignment, stepped profiles, softened rectangles, fluting, opaline globes, and material discipline.

## Application

1. Keep architecture and camera unless structural freedom is explicit.
2. Use one botanical gesture, not a pattern everywhere.
3. Use dark walnut, cream plaster, honed stone, ribbed glass, ivory mohair, cognac leather, black lacquer in tiny amounts, and restrained aged metal or stainless steel.
4. Coordinate a sculptural opaline chandelier, a tailored sofa, one club chair, and a softened rectangular stone table.
5. Use burgundy or deep tobacco as a small accent.
6. Keep decor sparse and residential.

## Avoid

Avoid gold overload, mirrored walls, fan motifs on every surface, black-and-gold luxury clichés, theatrical hotel styling, dense ornament, period replicas, round central coffee tables, and dim amber grading.
