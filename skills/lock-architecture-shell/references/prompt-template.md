# Architecture-lock prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit

Input image: binding construction and camera source.

Non-negotiable architecture lock:
- preserve exact camera, lens, crop, horizon, and perspective;
- preserve every wall position, thickness, length, and junction;
- preserve all doors, arches, windows, reveals, jambs, and openings;
- preserve beams, soffits, columns, ceiling planes, and height changes;
- preserve kitchen footprint, fixed service zones, floor boundaries, and circulation.

Editable scope: list only furniture, loose lighting, cabinet fronts, wall finish, floor finish, textiles, decor, planting, or rendering quality authorized by the user.

Do not move, resize, add, remove, hide, widen, narrow, or reinterpret any fixed architectural element.

For companion views: Image 1 is the geometry source for the target camera. Image 2 is only the approved design and material reference. Never let Image 2 override Image 1 geometry.

Reject outputs with a shifted arch, removed beam, changed ceiling level, relocated kitchen, new partition, enlarged opening, or altered camera.
