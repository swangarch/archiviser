# Quality restoration prompt

Use case: style-transfer

Primary request: completely re-render the approved image at premium photographic quality. This is a restoration pass, not a redesign.

Lock: camera, perspective, architecture, ceiling, furniture models and positions, palette, lighting concept, art, plants, and decor.

Rebuild:
- perfectly straight architectural edges and clean junctions;
- coherent timber grain and joinery;
- accurate herringbone board geometry;
- clean mineral plaster without noise or repeated embossing;
- fine textiles without moire;
- botanically credible foliage without fused leaves;
- correct furniture construction and floor contact;
- uniform TV screen and crisp bezel;
- physically believable global illumination and contact shadows.

Quality: natural sharpness, fine microcontrast, realistic material roughness, neutral white balance, normal saturation.

Avoid: design drift, painterly foliage, waxy materials, noisy walls, melted furniture, repeated textures, HDR, bloom, crunchy sharpening, chromatic aberration, text, logos, watermark.
