# Section-perspective prompt template

Use case: infographic-diagram.

Primary request: Translate the exact source into a human-eye architectural section-perspective.

Geometry authority: preserve crop, eye level, horizon, vanishing points, walls, openings, floor levels, stairs, fixed joinery, furniture layout, and program.

Cut plane: define one plausible vertical plane and list the walls, slabs, roof, stairs, or facade elements it intersects.

Line hierarchy:

- strongest restrained line and sparse hatch at actual cut material;
- medium-fine visible architecture beyond the cut;
- fine material joints and build-up conventions;
- lighter entourage silhouettes and finest internal detail.

Assemblies to reveal: name the finish, substrate, insulation or cavity, structure, lining, frame, fixing, and junctions that matter.

Detail enlargements: request zero to three exact nodes and link each to its source with a fine leader.

Graphic direction: white page, black linework, crisp drafting, correct perspective, ceilings mostly blank, perspective-correct flooring.

Avoid: axonometric conversion, flat orthographic section, arbitrary cut jumps, color, gradient, shadow, reflections, excessive poche, fake labels, dimensions, heavy entourage, text, logos, watermark.

Validation: compare camera, geometry, cut continuity, assembly order, material hierarchy, floor joints, entourage weight, and detail agreement with the source.
