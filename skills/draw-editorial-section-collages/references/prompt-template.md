# Editorial section collage prompt

Use case: style-transfer or precise-object-edit.

Input roles: identify the exact geometry source, edit target, material references, and entourage references.

Primary request: Translate the project into a precise editorial architectural collage while preserving the authoritative section, elevation, plan, or cutaway.

Geometry lock: preserve crop, projection, cut plane, walls, slabs, openings, levels, stairs, rails, structure, fixed joinery, furniture, planting zones, and program.

Drawing layer: crisp CAD-like linework; strongest flat poche only at actual cuts; medium visible architecture; lighter entourage.

Collage layers: name the clipped material fragments, paper tones, cut-out planting, people, halftone, photocopy grain, and one optional accent color. Require sharp controlled edges, deliberate print scale, and exact clipping to architectural planes.

Depth: use overlap, white paper edges, line weight, print density, and slight registration shifts. Do not simulate realistic light.

Composition: keep the complete architecture legible on a clean page with generous negative space and one clear spatial focus.

Avoid: seamless rendered materials, global illumination, ambient occlusion, smooth shadows, reflections, watercolor, uniform beige wash, scrapbook borders, random collage fragments, mood-board layout, bright color, text, dimensions, labels, logos, and watermark.

Validation: compare geometry and cut continuity, then inspect clipping, texture scale, entourage edges, palette, white space, and whether the result reads as collage rather than a render with paper grain.
