# Terrazzo chic prompt framework

Use case: style-transfer, sketch-to-render, or precise-object-edit.

Primary request: state the typology and whether the task is a full redesign, companion view, bar/café addition, furniture pass, or single material correction.

Geometry authority: lock camera, crop, walls, openings, stairs, landings, levels, ceiling, circulation, fixed joinery, and approved objects.

Terrazzo recipe: define base color, chip size, density, muted chip palette, sheen, panel joints, thresholds, nosings, and where it appears.

Supporting palette: define plaster, timber, glass, metal finish, textile and leather accents, and a rough neutral/accent balance.

Ondulé metal: name the rib or wave geometry, orientation, finish, scale, return edges, joints, and limited applications.

Furniture: specify a coherent family of chairs, stools, tables, benches, lounge pieces, and integrated seating with circulation clearances.

Bar or café operation when present: counter location, customer side, service depth, backbar, glass shelves, espresso or sink zone, undercounter storage, lighting, glassware, ceramics, bottles, staff, and stool count.

Small details: trays, cups, glasses, bowls, books, coasters, linen, menus without readable text, planting, and restrained occupants.

Lighting: warm-neutral daylight around 3500–4200K, soft bounce, controlled metal and terrazzo highlights, no yellow filter.

Multiview inventory: repeat the exact terrazzo recipe, wood tone, metal rib, glass, upholstery, lighting, stool and table models, joinery, and service details; let each target view control geometry.

Avoid: loud rainbow terrazzo, giant chips, Memphis, mirror chrome, metal overload, nightclub, shopping mall, generic café chain, crowded restaurant, black-and-gold luxury, blocked circulation, architecture drift, readable signs, logos, watermark.

Validation: compare geometry, aggregate recipe, material continuity, furniture scale, circulation, bar function, detail density, lighting, and cross-view consistency.
