# Public Interior Renderer

## Workflow

1. Preserve the spatial section, circulation, stairs, voids, seating geometry, structure, and camera.
2. Identify the program and show activity that proves it: reading, gathering, rehearsing, exhibiting, circulating, resting, or informal learning.
3. Improve material hierarchy instead of replacing every surface.
4. Use daylight to reveal depth. Keep shadows open and the white balance neutral.
5. Add acoustics, handrails, books, exhibition elements, benches, signage forms, plants, and personal objects only where programmatically credible.
6. Avoid empty museum-model space. Add enough scene detail to show occupation, but do not create crowd noise.
7. Validate stair proportions, rail continuity, bookshelves, brick joints, glass reflections, and human scale.

## Material guidance

- Brick: coherent coursing, mortar depth, edge wear, and color variation.
- Timber: species-consistent grain, joinery, and matte finish.
- Stone: large-scale mineral variation without dirty speckle.
- Concrete: subtle formwork or fine cast texture, never flat gray plastic.
- Glass: controlled reflections and visible thickness.
