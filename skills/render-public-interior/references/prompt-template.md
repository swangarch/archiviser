# Public interior prompt template

Use case: sketch-to-render

Primary request: preserve the exact spatial section, structure, stairs, voids, circulation, camera, and program while rebuilding the image as a photorealistic competition interior.

Program evidence:
- Library: books, reading lamps, quiet study, group learning, shelving depth.
- Museum: exhibition rhythm, benches, controlled graphics, visitors at varied distances.
- Pavilion: landscape continuity, light structure, informal occupation.
- Amphitheater: stepped seating, acoustic surfaces, performance or rehearsal activity.
- Brick circulation hall: handrails, art or artifacts, benches, directional light, small groups.

Lighting: bright neutral daylight with open shadows and believable falloff.

Materials: coherent joints, edge thickness, roughness, reflection, and wear.

People: anatomically plausible, varied scale, purposeful poses, restrained density.

Avoid: empty model space, pasted silhouettes, excessive props, yellow cast, gray desaturation, plastic surfaces, impossible railings, floating people, text, logos, watermark.
