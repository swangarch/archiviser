# Brick + timber prompt framework

Use case: sketch-to-render, style-transfer, or precise-object-edit.

Primary request: Design or refine a contemporary brick-and-timber project organized by credible masonry arches and a clear public program.

Geometry authority: identify the exact edit target and list the camera, walls, openings, levels, circulation, structure, and fixed elements to preserve.

Arch order: state the spatial axis, arch family, span, rise, springing line, pier width, arch-ring depth, repeated bay spacing, and whether bilateral symmetry is required.

Masonry logic: require visible wall thickness, plausible bearing, coherent brick bond, radial soffit joints, recessed mortar, clean reveals, and restrained color variation.

Timber role: assign timber to named secondary structure, ceiling or acoustic lining, shelving, doors, benches, tables, or other touch surfaces. Require credible spans, member sizes, orientation, and masonry connections.

Program: describe circulation, furniture, activity, lighting, and service elements that prove the building's use.

Lighting and palette: neutral daylight, open shadows, local warm-neutral light, nuanced low-saturation red brick, pale mineral floors, matte timber, and restrained metal or glass.

Avoid: asymmetric or distorted arches, mismatched springing points, thin decorative brick, impossible support, huge timber members, historical revival, church or warehouse clichés, rustic styling, orange cast, dirty materials, fragmented space, text, logos, and watermark.

Validation: compare the two sides of every axial arch; check bay repetition, bearing, brick direction, timber span and connection, circulation, furniture scale, and white balance.
