# Modern timber prompt framework

Use case: style-transfer, sketch-to-render, or precise-object-edit.

Primary request: state whether the task is a full timber redesign, a structural refinement, a craft and integrated-furniture pass, a joinery correction, or a single finish change.

Geometry authority: lock camera, crop, walls, openings, levels, stairs, ceiling, circulation, fixed joinery, and approved objects.

Typology and scale: name the program, span, height, occupancy, daylight, acoustic needs, and likely structural system. State whether the room is an ordinary post-and-beam span or requires an arch, truss, shell, or hybrid long-span solution.

Structural hierarchy:

- primary posts, beams, frames, panels, arches, or trusses;
- secondary joists, ribs, purlins, blocking, and enclosure frames;
- integrated shelving, stairs, guards, furniture, and services.

Visual weight: define a credible span-to-depth relationship, member hierarchy, bearing condition, and whether long-span depth should be open truss space rather than one oversized solid member.

Assembly language: specify two to four repeatable joint types, connector material, base and head conditions, tolerance gaps, and glass or mineral interfaces.

Craft and use: specify built-in shelves, tables, counters, benches, workshop or library functions, high-wear touch surfaces, accessibility, repairable details, and how each element aligns with the bay system.

Material palette: structural timber species and tone; floor or touch-surface timber; plaster, brick, stone, concrete, ceramic, linoleum, glass, metal, textiles; low-sheen finish.

Floor: state board size, laying axis, tone relationship, finish, thresholds, and patterns to avoid.

Lighting: neutral daylight, soft bounce, legible junctions, no yellow filter or glossy bloom.

Avoid: rustic cabin, cottage or historical craft revival, temple imitation, decorative brackets, all-over cladding, orange varnish, repeated grain, oversized solid members, impossible spans, random connectors, fake handmade irregularity, pattern clutter, architecture drift, text, logos, watermark.

Validation: compare geometry, load path, span-to-depth and member proportions, structural hierarchy, joint repetition, craft integration, furniture function, material interfaces, grain direction, floor grid, services, circulation, and realism.
