# Competition Exterior Renderer

## Workflow

1. Identify whether the source is massing-only or an exact geometry target.
2. Preserve silhouette, openings, circulation, bridges, water edges, and camera unless the user grants design freedom.
3. Choose day, post-sunset blue hour, or bright night from references/prompt-template.md.
4. Establish one dominant atmospheric idea and one material hierarchy.
5. Add believable context: vegetation, people, bicycles, local activity, paving, water reflections, furniture, signage without legible brand text, and weather traces.
6. Keep figures anatomically plausible and subordinate to architecture.
7. For rural projects, remove high-rise skylines and use fields, hills, village roofs, native planting, and open horizon.
8. For night scenes, keep facades and ground readable; use interior glow sparingly and avoid HDR halos.

## Competition character

- Prefer authored atmosphere over real-estate marketing polish.
- Use slight synthetic clarity only when it improves diagrammatic legibility.
- Maintain realistic saturation. Blue-hour backgrounds may be cool, but materials and planting must retain natural color.
- Let one or two areas carry detail; keep other zones calm.

Read references/prompt-template.md for reusable day and night prompt blocks.
