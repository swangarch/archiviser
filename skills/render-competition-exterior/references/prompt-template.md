# Competition exterior prompt templates

## Day view

Use case: sketch-to-render

Primary request: transform the supplied massing or collage into a high-end architectural competition exterior while preserving the building form, openings, camera, circulation, water edges, and landscape structure.

Atmosphere: bright overcast or soft late-afternoon daylight; readable shadows; natural sky; restrained contrast.

Materials: coherent brick, concrete, timber, glass, metal, stone, and paving with correct scale and weathering.

Scene: purposeful pedestrians, bicycles, seating, local planting, water activity, signage forms, and small traces of daily life. Use density selectively.

Color: natural saturation, neutral white balance, no yellow filter and no gray wash.

Avoid: commercial real-estate polish, cartoon figures, model appearance, empty plazas, excessive sharpness, fake high-rises, text, logos, watermark.

## Bright blue-hour or night view

Change only lighting, sky, activity, and practical-light state. Preserve architecture and materials.

Use a luminous post-sunset blue sky, readable facade exposure, restrained warm interiors, subtle path lighting, controlled reflections, and moderate activity. Keep shadows open. Avoid deep black facades, orange grading, HDR halos, dense glowing windows, and cinematic gloom.

## Rural context

Remove metropolitan skylines. Use distant fields, low hills, village roofs, native trees, hedges, gravel, wet ground, open horizon, and local everyday activity. Keep the project culturally significant without making it commercially monumental.
