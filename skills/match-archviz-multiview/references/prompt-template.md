# Multiview inventory and prompt

## Inventory

Record before generating:
- fixed architecture and opening dimensions;
- ceiling levels and fixtures;
- floor material and laying direction;
- wall and joinery materials;
- sofa, chair, table, and dining models;
- furniture positions and seat counts;
- TV wall and kitchen configuration;
- art, plants, curtains, and large decor;
- daylight direction and practical-light state.

## Prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit

Image 1: exact geometry and camera source for this view.

Image 2: approved design source for furniture, materials, palette, lighting fixtures, and styling.

Primary request: create the companion or reverse view of exactly the same project.

Preserve Image 1 architecture. Translate Image 2 design through the new camera. Match every inventory item where visible. Do not mirror screen positions mechanically.

Validate: openings, ceiling levels, floor direction, sofa model, chair count, table shape, joinery, TV wall, kitchen, curtains, art, plant positions, and palette.

Generate each requested view as a separate image.
