# Nordic mineral prompt framework

Use case: style-transfer, sketch-to-render, or precise-object-edit.

Primary request: state the typology and whether the task is a full redesign, companion view, material refinement, furniture pass, or single correction.

Geometry authority: lock camera, crop, walls, openings, roof, levels, stairs, circulation, fixed joinery, and approved objects.

Climate and landscape: define coastal, harbor, island, forest, snow, low-sun, overcast, or twilight conditions and how openings mediate them.

Spatial order: identify the principal room, continuous planes, wall thickness, deep openings, thresholds, benches, service zones, and clear circulation.

Mineral hierarchy: define the principal granite, slate, brick, concrete, or stone; its module, finish, joints, edges, thickness, thresholds, and clean or weathered state.

Supporting palette: define cool-toned timber, brushed metal, glass, wool, linen, leather, ceramics, and a restrained low-saturation color range.

Furniture and use: specify a coherent furniture family, integrated seating, tables, service elements, occupants, and required clearances.

Lighting: describe northern daylight around 4200–4800K, local warm-neutral task light around 3000–3500K, wall grazing, clean whites, and controlled shadow detail.

Avoid: generic Scandinavian styling, hygge decor, cabin or sauna cues, luxury resort, all-over timber, dirty gray surfaces, arbitrary stone features, gloomy exposure, yellow or blue cast, empty shell, clutter, architecture drift, readable text, logos, and watermark.

Validation: compare geometry, spatial continuity, mineral module and finish, surface cleanliness, material hierarchy, climate response, furniture scale, lighting balance, circulation, and realism.
